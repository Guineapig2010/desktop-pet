Chat AI

Without an AI service he still works: wandering, chasing and the scripted Albuquerque conversations. The chat panel is then clearly marked Offline · scripted.

Service

Needs your own key from console.anthropic.com. Billed per use by Anthropic: Claude Opus 5.5 is $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, so a typical chat reply costs well under a cent. Cheaper option: claude-haiku-5-5 ($0.10 / $0.50 per million).

Free and local: install Ollama, run ollama pull llama3.2, then use base URL http://localhost:11434/v1 (no key needed). Hosted services bill per use on your own account.

Keys are encrypted with your Mac's Keychain (Electron safeStorage) and stored only on this computer. They are never shown again, never put in the app's code, and only sent to the service you pick.

Personal Helper

Share ONE display or window from the chat panel (👁 Share screen…). He then answers questions about it, points at things, or — in Do it mode — flies over with his jetpack and really clicks, scrolls and types. A red bar with a Stop button shows whenever he can see your screen. Esc cancels him instantly. He asks before sending, buying, deleting or submitting anything.

Screenshots are sent to your chosen AI service only when you ask him something about your screen (or on every message while Live is on). The chat notes each time. Clicking and typing need Claude (Anthropic); OpenAI-compatible vision models can explain only.
